Ask on WhatsApp
What are the models made of?

Our models are premium die-cast alloy with finely detailed parts — they have real weight and a quality finish that plastic toys can't match.

Do they come assembled?

Most models arrive ready to display — just place them on the included stand. Some items are assembly kits for hobbyists who enjoy building; these are clearly marked “Assembly kit” on the product page.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 days and usually arrive in 3–5 working days nationwide. You'll receive tracking details on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery is available nationwide. You can also pay by bank transfer or JazzCash / Easypaisa — choose your preferred option at checkout.

What if my model arrives damaged?

Every model is foam-packed, but if anything arrives damaged, send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you.

Do you make great gifts?

Absolutely — they're a hit for birthdays, retirements, military & aviation enthusiasts and executive desks. Ask us about gift notes.
